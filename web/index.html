Scoring notes

The five HEART components — history, ECG, age, risk factors and initial troponin — contribute 0–2 points each (total 0–10).

HEART categories: 0–3 low, 4–6 intermediate, and 7–10 high risk. The Pathway classification additionally requires a HEAR score ≤3, negative serial troponins, a nonischemic ECG and no prior CAD.

Privacy: Calculations occur locally in JavaScript. Inputs are not sent to a server, retained in browser storage, or included in analytics. This site does not collect identifiers.
